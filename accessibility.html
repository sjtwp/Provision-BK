Accessibility Statement

Provision Bookkeeping LLC

Last Updated: November 17, 2026

Provision Bookkeeping LLC wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use our website and learn about our services. We work to make provisionbk.com conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What We Have Done

  • Images have text descriptions (alt text) for screen readers.
  • Buttons, links and menus have names that screen readers can announce, and the menus can be opened with a keyboard.
  • Form fields have labels.
  • Each page has a “Skip to main content” link and a visible outline that shows where you are when you move through the page with a keyboard.
  • Text colors have been checked for enough contrast against their backgrounds.
  • The rotating client reviews can be paused, and they do not rotate on their own for visitors whose device is set to reduce motion.

Known Limitations

Some content comes from other companies, such as our scheduling tool (Microsoft Bookings), Google reviews and maps, and our form service (Formspree). We choose providers that support accessibility, but we do not control their pages. Some downloadable documents on our Free Resources page may not be fully accessible yet. If you need any document in another format, just ask and we will provide it.

Need Help or Found a Problem?

If anything on our website is hard to use, please let us know and we will help you get the information or service you need another way, such as by phone or email. We also want to fix the problem.
