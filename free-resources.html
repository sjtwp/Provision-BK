New Hire & Vendor Forms

These links go straight to the official government pages, so you always get the current version.

IRS

Form W-4

Every new employee fills this out for federal tax withholding. The IRS updates it every year.

Get the W-4
Arizona

Arizona Form A-4

Arizona employees choose their state withholding rate on this form. New hires have 5 days to turn it in.

Get the A-4
USCIS

Form I-9

Verifies every new employee can work in the U.S. You can fill it in on screen, but it must be printed and signed by hand.

Get the I-9
IRS

Form W-9

Get one from every contractor or vendor before you pay them. You'll need it for 1099s at year-end.

Get the W-9

Keep completed forms in a secure place. Don't send the W-4, A-4 or I-9 to the IRS or the state unless they ask for them.
