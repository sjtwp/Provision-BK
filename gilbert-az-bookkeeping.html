Arizona tax and licensing in Gilbert

Arizona's sales tax is called transaction privilege tax (TPT). If your Gilbert business sells taxable goods or services, you collect and file state, Maricopa County and Town of Gilbert portions on one return through the Arizona Department of Revenue at AZTaxes.gov, monthly, quarterly or annually depending on your volume.

A lot of Gilbert owners hold rental homes. Since January 1, 2025, Arizona no longer allows city TPT on long-term residential rentals of 30 days or more, but short-term rentals under 30 days and commercial rentals are still taxable. Contractors working in Gilbert pay prime contracting TPT on new construction but generally not on repair and remodel (MRRA) work. We set up your books so each of these is tracked separately and your returns match your records.
