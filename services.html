Bookkeeping Services

QuickBooks Online bookkeeping for Arizona small businesses

Provision Bookkeeping is a QuickBooks ProAdvisor bookkeeping firm based in Gilbert, Arizona. Plans start at $250 per month, and every plan comes with a 60-day money-back guarantee.

More than a bookkeeper

We're more than a bookkeeper who keeps your books current. Every month we look at your numbers and tell you what they mean: where margins are slipping, which costs are creeping up, and practical ideas to make and save more money. Think of us as a light fractional CFO, at a bookkeeping price.

We also set up your books for how your industry works, whether you run a construction company, own rentals, flip houses or run a medical or dental practice. See the industries we serve.

Monthly Bookkeeping

Your books, done every month. We categorize every transaction, reconcile every bank, credit card and loan account, and send you clean financial statements, so you never have to wonder whether your numbers are right.

What's included

  • Categorizing all bank and credit card transactions in QuickBooks Online
  • Monthly reconciliation of every bank, credit card and loan account
  • Recording loans, owner contributions and owner draws correctly
  • A monthly Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet
  • Questions flagged to you in one short list instead of scattered emails

Best for: owners who want accurate books every month without doing them. Plans start at $250 per month.

Catch-Up & Cleanup Bookkeeping

Behind by a few months or a few years? Books that were set up wrong? We fix miscategorized transactions, remove duplicates, reconcile every account back to the bank statements and correct opening balances so your history is accurate and tax-ready.

What's included

  • A review of where your books stand today and a clear plan to fix them
  • Catching up every missing month and reconciling each account
  • Fixing opening balances, duplicate entries and uncategorized transactions
  • Clean year-end reports for your CPA or tax preparer

Best for: anyone facing a tax deadline, a loan application or a pile of unreconciled accounts. Once you're caught up, you can move to monthly bookkeeping or keep going on your own.

QuickBooks Online Setup & Training

Start QuickBooks Online the right way. A certified ProAdvisor sets up your company file, chart of accounts, bank feeds and bank rules, enters your opening balances, and then shows you how to keep it up yourself.

What's included

  • Company settings and a chart of accounts built for your industry
  • Connecting bank and credit card feeds and setting up bank rules
  • Opening balances and any history you need brought in
  • Moving from QuickBooks Desktop to QuickBooks Online
  • One-on-one training on the tasks you'll do every week

Best for: new businesses, owners switching from QuickBooks Desktop or spreadsheets, and do-it-yourselfers who want a solid starting point. Prefer to try it yourself first? Read our QuickBooks Online setup guide.

Payroll & 1099 Services

We make sure your people get paid correctly and your payroll shows up correctly in your books. We work with QuickBooks Payroll and connect or check the payroll system you already use.

What's included

  • Payroll setup, including Arizona withholding (Form A-4) and new-hire paperwork
  • Recording wages, payroll taxes and liabilities correctly each pay period
  • Tracking contractors through the year and issuing 1099s in January

Best for: businesses with employees or contractors. Need the forms? Download fillable W-4, W-9, A-4 and I-9 forms from our free resources.
